“During my time at my partial hospitalization program, one of our activities was to write down on paper something you have lost in your life, rip it up, and then make artwork out of it! That has taught me to view my grief as a way to help me throughout my mental health journey. You can see how that is expressed in this piece because my loss protects my heart.” —Anonymous
