Submit an article

Share your writing.

We publish student writing on mental health, wellness, and teen life. Whether you're sharing personal experiences or practical advice, your voice can make a difference.

✨

Earn Community Service Hours

Published articles may be eligible for up to 25 community service hours, awarded based on the quality, originality, depth, and impact of the submission.

Submission guidelines

Topic

Mental health, wellness, coping strategies, or teen life. Personal essays welcome.

Length

Minimum 600 words.

Tone

Write in your own voice. Be honest, clear, and specific.

Originality

Submissions must be original work written by the student; AI-generated or AI-written content will not be accepted.

Format

Paste your text below, or share a link to a Google Doc.

Review

Every submission is reviewed by our Content Team. If your article is selected, we'll typically contact you within 1–2 weeks before publication. Anonymous submissions are also welcome.

Why Contribute?

Share your perspective, help educate others, and become part of a growing community dedicated to supporting youth mental health. Selected articles may be featured on our website and social media.

We value authenticity over perfection. Write in your own voice—we'll help with grammar and formatting if needed.
